---
title: "Full-copy syndication beats curation"
type: claim
tags: ["publishing", "social-media"]
by: "Andy Smith"
status: "tentative"
---

A mirror channel should get a full, unfiltered copy of everything rather than a curated subset, because a full copy takes zero effort from the author and simplicity wins; curation can wait until the channel grows enough to justify the work.

Andy's reasoning about Telegram — "I lean toward a full copy being simpler, since it takes no effort from me" — treats effort as the binding constraint on a one-person publishing pipeline: any filtering scheme adds per-post labor that will silently stop happening. It sits inside [[POSSE (indieweb.org)|POSSE]] (own site as hub, copies downstream) but takes a side POSSE does not: syndicate *everything*, accept the noise, curate later — if ever.
