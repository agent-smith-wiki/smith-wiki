---
title: "Microsandbox"
type: concept
---

A tool, described at https://microsandbox.dev/, that runs [[OCI Image]]s inside a [[MicroVM]] on Windows, Linux, and Apple Silicon **without requiring Docker**.

Its whole VM lifecycle — build, launch, run, teardown — is scriptable as code, which makes a disposable computer cheap enough to allocate per agent or per task. This is the enabling primitive for [[Per-Task Agent Sandboxing]].

Ingested via [[Microsandbox: A Computer for Anything, Anywhere]].
