A small, fast [[Agent harness]] that speaks the [[Agent Client Protocol]], so an ACP-speaking client can spawn it without bespoke glue. Positioned as a lighter substitute for the larger [[omp]] harness inside [[Zeno]]: swapping it in is a harness-level choice rather than a rewrite of the orchestrator.
