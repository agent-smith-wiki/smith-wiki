Running a company on agents.

**The idea:** an [[AI-native company]] built on the [[AI-native company stack]] — communication, orchestration ([[Agent orchestrator]]), infrastructure ([[nixops]]) — and, at the top, [[Reflection.dev]] as the framework that [[Describe a company as code|describes the whole company as code]].

**The frame:** an organization is a single assembly line with its own context and its own agents, not a bundle of individual craftsmen ([[An organization is one assembly line, not many craftsmen]]), evolving under rules humans set ([[Self-evolving agent orchestrator]]).

**How it runs:** [[Deterministic process, stochastic where needed]], with [[Bounded context]] deciding when a unit becomes its own instance, and an [[LLM gateway]] as the model-access layer.

**Where the work lives:** [[The log is the workspace]], [[Agent identity is a keypair]], [[Branch-as-channel]].

**Positions:** [[Agents should be team-scoped, not personal]], [[One orchestrator instance can run a whole company]], [[A living runtime compounds work into code]], [[Security is structural, not bolted on]], [[A chat agent is low-value next to one that does the work]], [[Working code beats design docs]].

**Open questions:** [[Does company as code repeat the failures of business process management?]].
