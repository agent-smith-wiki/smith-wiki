This 2022 paper defines attributed question answering as producing an answer paired with evidence that supports it. It proposes human attribution judgments as the gold standard and evaluates an automatic metric that correlates with those judgments, separating answer quality from whether the evidence actually substantiates the answer.

Relevance: it supplies a reproducible framework for testing claim–evidence alignment instead of equating a citation's presence with attribution. Because the automatic evaluator is calibrated against, rather than identical to, human judgment, the framework also supports retaining human review for consequential claims.
