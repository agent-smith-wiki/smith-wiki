Google advises publishers to create for an intended audience, add original analysis or value, provide clear sourcing and authorship, and avoid mass-producing summaries merely to capture search traffic. For substantially automated or AI-generated work, it recommends explaining who created it, how automation was used and why it was useful.

Relevance: an AI lab should disclose AI assistance and make each question page independently useful rather than publishing a volume-driven feed. This is first-party guidance from a dominant discovery platform, not independent evidence about what readers value, and it partly reflects Google's own product incentives.
