A distributed object programming environment at the center of Spritely. [[Goblins]] combines [[Goblins local transactions]] for locally synchronous operations with [[Location-transparent asynchronous object interaction]] for [[Encapsulated object|encapsulated objects]] that may live elsewhere on the network.

Its advertised integrated model also includes security, [[Goblins distributed debugging]], [[Capability-preserving process persistence and upgrade]], and [[Cross-language distributed objects]]. Guile and Racket implementations were both listed at version 0.18.0 in the source. The project page states these capabilities but does not establish performance, security, or reliability relative to other distributed runtimes.
