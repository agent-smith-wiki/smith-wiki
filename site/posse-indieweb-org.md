POSSE — Publish (on your) Own Site, Syndicate Elsewhere — the IndieWeb's practice of posting content on your own domain first, then publishing copies or links to third-party silos, with permalinks back to the original so readers have a path to the owned version. It is a response to platform lock-in: the own site is the durable archive that survives shutdowns and policy changes, and comments from silos can even be backfed to it (e.g. via Bridgy/Webmentions). The inverse pattern is PESOS — Publish Elsewhere, Syndicate to Your Own Site — where the silo is the origin and the own site becomes the archive.

Relevance: Andy Smith's channel layout (blog as hub, Telegram as a full mirror, everything else downstream) is a POSSE-shaped strategy; his addition is giving each syndication target a different function rather than a plain copy — see [[Channel-differentiated publishing]].
