Centaur was fine-tuned on Psych-101, a dataset of more than 10 million trial-by-trial choices from 60,092 participants across 160 experiments. It predicted held-out participants' choices better than established cognitive models and generalized to new cover stories, task structures, and domains.

This demonstrates broad behavioral prediction from human choice data, not a clone of one person. The model is correlational and trained across a population; predictive similarity does not establish that it shares human cognitive mechanisms, beliefs, or memories.
