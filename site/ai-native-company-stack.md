The layers an [[AI-native company]] runs on: a communication layer where humans and agents talk over any channel, an orchestration layer that runs the processes and spawns agents (an [[Agent orchestrator]]), and an infrastructure layer you own ([[nixops]]). Each is a small tool adoptable on its own.
