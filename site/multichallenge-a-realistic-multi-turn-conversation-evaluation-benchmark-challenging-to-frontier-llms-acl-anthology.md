MultiChallenge is an ACL 2025 benchmark for realistic multi-turn conversations requiring instruction following, context allocation, and in-context reasoning together. Its four challenge categories include instruction retention and inference over information distributed through a dialogue. The benchmark uses instance-specific rubrics and reports agreement between automated judging and experienced human raters.

Despite near-perfect scores on earlier multi-turn benchmarks, every frontier model evaluated scored below 50% on MultiChallenge. The best reported model, Claude 3.5 Sonnet (October 2024), averaged 41.4%. These figures describe the tested 2024-era model snapshots and should not be generalized directly to newer systems, but they establish that fluent multi-turn interaction does not imply dependable context use.
