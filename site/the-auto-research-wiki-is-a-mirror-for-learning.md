The agent-written [[Smith Wiki|wiki]] is used in two ways the author would not trust his own writing for: as a mirror giving a bird's-eye view of where he is actually heading, and as a scaffold for grasping a difficult article — when the agent generates pages from what he already knows, the new material becomes easier to understand. He does not take responsibility for these texts.

Both uses depend on the pages being grounded in the author's own corpus rather than the open web — the [[An auto-researcher built on my blog (andysmith.ai)|auto-researcher]] reads the blog/notes and writes into the wiki — so the synthesis arrives already in his vocabulary: a personalized instance of [[Knowledge Synthesis]] built on [[Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) (en.wikipedia.org)|Retrieval-Augmented Generation]]. Open question: whether a mirror the author disclaims still counts as his own view — the standing cost of the public-first corpus ([[What does a knowledge base lose when it is written public-first?]]).
