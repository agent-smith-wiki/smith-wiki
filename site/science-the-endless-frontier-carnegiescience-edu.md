Retrospective on Vannevar Bush's report *Science—The Endless Frontier*, submitted July 5, 1945 in response to Roosevelt's 1944 request for advice on peacetime science policy. The report's core arguments: scientific research is vital to the country's continued economic well-being and security; basic research is "the pacemaker of technological progress"; new products and processes are "founded on new principles and new conceptions that result from basic scientific research." Bush's proposed centralized, government-supported research enterprise led to the creation of the National Science Foundation and set postwar US science policy.
