Bus factor (also truck factor, or in business terms key-person risk) is the minimum number of team members who would have to disappear — hit by the proverbial bus — before a project stalls for lack of the knowledge they hold. A bus factor of 1 means a single person is critical; studies of popular open-source projects find a large share sitting at a factor of 1 or 2, and recovery from losing a key engineer is measured in months.

Relevance: the established name for the risk in Andy's factory analogy — "what happens if he leaves the company?" when work is produced inside one person's (or one agent's) invisible process. It grounds [[Invisible agent processes are a factory-floor defect]].
