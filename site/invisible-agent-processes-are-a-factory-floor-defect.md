---
title: "Invisible agent processes are a factory-floor defect"
type: claim
by: "Andy Smith"
status: "tentative"
---

Software development runs, everywhere, on the kind of process no factory would tolerate. Andy's image: at a car factory, if every fitter took a blank home in the evening and brought back a finished part in the morning, nobody would know how it was made, nobody could judge the quality of the process, hidden defects would be undetectable from the result, and the knowledge would walk out with the fitter. Personal agents make the process *more* invisible, not less — a work-in-progress done inside an agent's head that never touches the shared context is a [[Bus factor (en.wikipedia.org)|bus-factor]] of 1 wrapped in a black box. The antidote is shared observable process: [[The log is the workspace]] and [[Work with the garage door up (notes.andymatuschak.org)|work with the garage door up]].
