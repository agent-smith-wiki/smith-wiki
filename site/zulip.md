Team chat organized as streams (channels) and topics (threads), with first-class bots. Chosen as the communication layer for the auto-researcher: quick to write, good for long messages, and easy to add agents — a natural home for [[Thread subscription]].

On Andy's research platform it is also the private chat where he thinks with his agents, and it is written as a [[Pseudo-public workspace|pseudo-public]] space — as if it will leak.
