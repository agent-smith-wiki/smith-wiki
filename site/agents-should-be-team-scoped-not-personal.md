---
title: "Agents should be team-scoped, not personal"
type: claim
by: "Andy Smith"
status: "tentative"
---

The 'everyone drives their own agent, then merges the results' model breaks the moment a team moves fast: divergent context, duplicated work, and conflicts to reconcile by hand. Better to give the team one shared context where agents work in the open — team agents, not personal ones, with nothing to merge.
