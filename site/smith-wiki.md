`smith.wiki` — the domain Andy Smith bought (two years for a bit over $100, which he calls a steal) as the intended home for the output of an [[Auto-Researcher]] built on his public notes.

It is the output surface rather than the corpus: research produced by the agent lands here, while the agent reads his blog/notes. See [[Public-First Note-Writing]] for the constraint under which that corpus is maintained.
