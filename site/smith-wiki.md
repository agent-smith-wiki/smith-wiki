`smith.wiki` — the domain Andy Smith bought (two years for a bit over $100, which he calls a steal) as the intended home for the output of an [[Auto-Researcher]] built on his public notes.

It is the output surface rather than the corpus: research produced by the agent lands here, while the agent reads his blog/notes. This mirrors STORM ([[STORM: Writing Wikipedia-like Articles From Scratch (arxiv.org)|STORM: Writing Wikipedia-like Articles From Scratch]]), which autonomously produces Wikipedia-like articles, but here the articles are grounded in one author's public notes rather than the open Internet. Bush's [[Memex (en.wikipedia.org)|Memex]] likewise imagined that from a private, annotated file would come "wholly new forms of encyclopedias... a mesh of associative trails" — the wiki as the projected public artifact of a personal corpus. See [[Public-First Note-Writing]] for the constraint under which that corpus is maintained.
