Liu, Zhang and Liang had human annotators evaluate Bing Chat, NeevaAI, Perplexity and YouChat on diverse information-seeking queries collected in early 2023. Across systems, responses averaged 4.48/5 for fluency and 4.50/5 for perceived usefulness, yet only 51.5% of generated statements were fully supported by citations and 74.5% of citations supported their associated statement.

Relevance: fluent AI research with citations can create a facade of trustworthiness. Claim-level sourcing and human verification are necessary publication practices. These measurements are a time-bound audit of four 2023 products, not a current benchmark for all models or a finding that AI-assisted research is inherently unreliable.
