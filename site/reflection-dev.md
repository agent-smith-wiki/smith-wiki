An infrastructure framework for [[AI-native company|AI-native companies]], shipped as a product on top of [[Zeno]]. Where Zeno is the orchestrator runtime, Reflection is the layer that lets you describe any company as code — [[Company as code]]: the [[Agent orchestrator]], its agents, their roles and the rules of evolution live in one definition, and [[Conversation is the company's control plane|people steer the whole thing from chat]]. [[The orchestrator can create agents itself]], and each agent's access to shared context is [[Role-gated access to shared context|role-gated]] in its sandbox, with the company definition written in Lisp so it can [[Company-as-code wants a homoiconic language|grow as its own agents produce code]]. The post frames it as a weekend project for someone who runs NixOS fleets by day and writes Lisp at night — [[AI-native company stack|infrastructure thinking]] applied to the organization itself.
