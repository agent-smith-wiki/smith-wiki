---
title: "Company as code"
type: concept
by: "Andy Smith"
---

Describing an organization — its [[Agent orchestrator]], its agents, their roles, and the rules by which it evolves — as a versioned, declarative definition, the way [[Infrastructure as code (en.wikipedia.org)|infrastructure as code]] describes servers. The orchestrator runs the definition as its configuration, and the organization's fleet is generated from it rather than assembled by hand, so an [[AI-native company]] gets IaC's guarantees — reproducibility, reviewability, idempotent application — applied to *who* works and *how*, not just *what* runs. It licenses treating the company as [[An organization is a single pipeline, not a bundle of individuals|a single pipeline]]: one declaration instead of many hand-coordinated private processes.
