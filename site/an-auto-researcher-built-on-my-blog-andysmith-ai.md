Digest: Andy Smith announces [[Smith Wiki]] — he finally bought the domain `smith.wiki` (two years, ~$100) to host the output of an [[Auto-Researcher]]: an agent that builds research out of his own accumulated notes.

Key ideas:
- The auto-researcher is a long-standing idea in his head; the domain purchase is the trigger to give its output a home.
- He originally imagined a private note corpus plus a filtered public wiki, and wrestled with the [[Private/Public Note Split]]. He now treats a reliable split as pretty much impossible — an agent will let something private slip through eventually.
- The workaround: write only public notes (he still self-filters at authorship time), and give the agent the public corpus only, with no access to private material. See [[Public-First Note-Writing]].

Implication: privacy becomes a property of what is written, not of a downstream filter — which is what makes an autonomous agent acceptable here.
