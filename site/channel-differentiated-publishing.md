---
title: "Channel-differentiated publishing"
type: concept
tags: ["publishing", "social-media"]
by: "Andy Smith"
---

Giving each platform in a publishing strategy a distinct job instead of posting the same thing everywhere: Zulip for thinking with agents, the blog for pinning thoughts down, Telegram as an unfiltered mirror, Smith.wiki for auto-research, X/Bluesky for technical detail, Threads for accessible questions, Instagram for reels. Selection doubts such as LinkedIn ("between X and Threads") then become a question of *which job* a platform should hold.

Aligns with the indieweb practice of [[POSSE (indieweb.org)|POSSE]] in that the blog is the owned hub and the other channels are syndication from it — but extends it: each syndication target is not a copy but a different medium doing a different function, so what gets said on a channel is shaped by what that platform amplifies.
