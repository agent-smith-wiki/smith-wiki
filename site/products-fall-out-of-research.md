The venture is an AI lab, not a product startup: research comes first, and products fall out of the research process on their own — how they fall out is itself part of the research. On this view the whole publishing strategy is upstream of products: public output is how the lab builds "weight in the community."

This is the priority Vannevar Bush argued for in *Science—The Endless Frontier* ([[Science, the Endless Frontier (carnegiescience.edu)|Science, the Endless Frontier]]): basic research is "the pacemaker of technological progress" and new products are grounded in new principles from basic research. It also fits [[Pasteur's quadrant (en.wikipedia.org)|Pasteur's quadrant]] in that the lab's research has application in view from the start — but it extends both: the productization step is itself treated as a research problem rather than a downstream engineering one. See [[AI-native companies]] for the agent-run company this lab is.
