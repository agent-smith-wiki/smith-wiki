title: "Humans set the rules of an organization's evolution"
type: claim
by: "Andy Smith"

---

The organization can evolve — [[The orchestrator can create agents itself]] and roles [[Role-gated access to shared context|change on the fly]] — but the *rules* of that evolution are written by people, once, in the [[Company as code|company definition]]. Evolution is a governed [[Supervised loop]], not free self-modification: what an agent may concede or change is a human decision expressed in code. It aligns with [[Deterministic process, stochastic where needed]] — the stochastic parts (agent output) stay contained inside a deterministic frame that a human set.
