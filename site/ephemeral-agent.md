---
title: "Ephemeral agent"
type: concept
by: "Andy Smith"
---

An agent that exists only for a single task and holds no state of its own: its state is its sandbox plus its working dirs, and anything durable is written to external artifacts. It is created (or revived) with a message, does the work, and is torn down.
