Planned pattern for Threads: questions phrased so a non-technical person can follow, with an agent showing up to answer them from the researched [[Smith Wiki|wiki]] and the author debating the answers publicly — "a kind of public discussion." The agent does not ghost-write for him; it argues from researched material the author can stand next to or against in the open.

This extends the [[Auto-Researcher]] from an output surface into the social layer. Because the corpus and the private chat are already written [[Pseudo-public workspace|as if public]], an agent speaking publicly cannot leak material that was not already publishable — a consequence of the pseudo-public discipline rather than of any filter on the agent.
