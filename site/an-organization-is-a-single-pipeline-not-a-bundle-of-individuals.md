---
title: "An organization is a single pipeline, not a bundle of individuals"
type: claim
by: "Andy Smith"
status: "tentative"
---

An organization is a thing in its own right: one pipeline with its own context and its own set of agents, not a bunch of people each doing a part of the work. Treating a company as a single running system is what licenses [[Company as code]] — one declaration rather than many hand-coordinated private processes. It complements [[One orchestrator instance can run a whole company]] (scale-out only at [[Bounded context|trust or autonomy boundaries]]) and [[Agents should be team-scoped, not personal]]. Where Conway's Law says the organization's communication structure will be copied into the systems it designs, this claim makes that structure an explicit, designed pipeline instead of an accident of individuals.
