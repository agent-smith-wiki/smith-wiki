---
title: "A chat agent is low-value next to one that does the work"
type: claim
by: "Andy Smith"
status: "tentative"
---

An agent you only chat with — ask a question, get an answer — adds a separate chore. The valuable agent does real work with you, and research and knowledge capture happen automatically as a byproduct: sources, findings and decisions land in the wiki as the work proceeds.
