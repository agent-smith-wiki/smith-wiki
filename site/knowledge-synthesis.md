title: "Knowledge Synthesis"
type: concept
tags: ["knowledge-synthesis", "research", "literature-review"]
---

The work of integrating relevant theories, concepts, claims and evidence into novel conceptual wholes — producing a theory, an integrative or systematic literature review, a causal model, a problem formulation or a design space, rather than merely summarising sources.

Chan ([[Discourse Graphs for Augmented Knowledge Synthesis (joelchan.me)|Discourse Graphs for Augmented Knowledge Synthesis]]) argues synthesis is critical to scientific advance but scarce and laborious, and that a main obstacle is the *information model*: documents and their metadata do not expose the level at which synthesis happens — individual claims and their evidentiary context. His proposed remedy is the [[Discourse Graph]].

The meetup ([[Discourse graphs at the Tinderbox meetup (forum.eastgate.com)|Discourse graphs at the Tinderbox meetup]]) frames synthesis as both a personal (PKM) and collaborative (CKM) practice, supported by shared conventions for capturing questions, claims and evidence.

Related: an [[Auto-Researcher]] is a machine attempt at the same task — producing new integrative output from a corpus. See [[Could an auto-researcher build the discourse graph?]].
