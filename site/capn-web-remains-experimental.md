Cloudflare’s launch article describes [[Cap’n Web]] as new and highly experimental and warns prospective adopters to expect bugs. Its quantitative packaging claim has also drifted: the article reported a minified-and-gzipped implementation under 10 kB, while the current repository describes the library as under 16 kB with no dependencies.

The stable conclusion is that the project is dependency-free and small for its feature set; a precise bundle-size number is version-specific rather than an enduring property.
