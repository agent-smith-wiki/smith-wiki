---
title: "AI-native company"
type: concept
by: "Andy Smith"
---

A company whose processes are run by agents rather than only by people: work is described explicitly, agents execute it, ask when they need a human, and capture what they learn. The stack for building one has three layers — communication, orchestration, and infrastructure.
