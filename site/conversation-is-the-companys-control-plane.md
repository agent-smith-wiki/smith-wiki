---
title: "Conversation is the company's control plane"
type: concept
by: "Andy Smith"
---

In an [[AI-native company]], the team's chat is the control plane: people discuss ideas, assign tasks to agents, and answer agents' questions in the same channels the agents inhabit, while the company's agents do all the work. A thread is where work is requested and steered — [[Thread subscription]] is how an agent is invoked there, [[Ask-user]] is how it suspends on a question and resumes with the answer, and [[Branch-as-channel]] keeps that conversation and its artifacts reviewable. Because steering happens in conversation rather than tickets or pull requests, the tasking surface is the same one human collaboration already uses. This does not conflict with [[A chat agent is low-value next to one that does the work]]: conversation *commands* work, it does not substitute for it — the valuable part is the agent that does the work, with the chat thread as the shared log of why.
