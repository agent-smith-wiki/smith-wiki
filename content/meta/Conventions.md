The standing contract for every card in this wiki. Tasks link here instead of
restating it; the worker follows it on every run.

## Sections

- `concepts/` — encyclopedic concept cards.
- `connections/` — cards bridging Andy's notes to concepts.
- `references/` — cited sources.
- `meta/` — wiki rules like this one.

## Card kinds

- **concept** — an encyclopedic, objective, canonical explanation of a
  well-established idea. Evergreen; created once and reused; no mention of Andy
  or his notes. The answer to "what is X".
- **connection** — a small, separate card bridging a specific claim in one of
  Andy's notes to a concept, or flagging a possible misinterpretation, judged
  objectively against the concept. Many connections may point at one concept.
- **reference** — a source cited by other cards.

## Rules

- **Atomic.** One idea per card; if it needs two, make two and link them.
- **Objective.** Established knowledge only on concept cards; Andy's angle lives
  on connection cards, kept distinct.
- **Cited.** Every non-obvious claim is grounded in a fetched source; concept
  cards cite at least two.
- **Linked.** Dense [[wikilinks]]; link related cards instead of duplicating them.
- **Canonical.** Never rewrite a concept card for a new angle — add a connection
  card instead.
- **Markdown is the source of truth;** the knowledge graph is derived from it.
- **Leave the garden tidy.** Prune broken links; keep front matter minimal
  (title, type, tags).
