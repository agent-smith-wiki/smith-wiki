An **orchestrator** is the coordination component of a [[Multi-agent system]] that binds autonomous [[AI agent|agents]] into one goal-directed collective. It receives a high-level objective, decomposes it into subtasks, routes each to a suitable specialist agent, manages the shared state and message flow between them, and aggregates their outputs. It may be implemented as a dedicated software layer or as a designated supervisory agent.
