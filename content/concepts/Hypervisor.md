A hypervisor, also called a virtual machine monitor (VMM), is the software, firmware or hardware layer that creates and runs [[Virtual machine]]s, presenting each guest operating system with a virtual hardware platform and keeping guests isolated from one another (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypervisor).
