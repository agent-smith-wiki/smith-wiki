**Hyprland** is a free, open-source dynamic [[Tiling window manager]] and [[Wayland]] compositor written in C++ and released under the BSD 3-Clause licence. Where conventional desktops separate the window manager from the display server, Hyprland merges both roles in one process, offering automatic window tiling with highly customisable, animated visuals. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyprland https://github.com/hyprwm/Hyprland https://wiki.archlinux.org/title/Hyprland
