An experience lake is a single person's complete longitudinal record of experience — the events they encounter together with the reactions those events provoke — held as a [[Knowledge graph]] under their own [[Data ownership]] and readable slice by slice through [[Selective disclosure]] rather than only as a whole. It is the life-spanning case of a [[Personal data store]].
