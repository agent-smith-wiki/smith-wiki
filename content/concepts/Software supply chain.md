title: Software supply chain

description: The chain of activities, organizations, and components carrying software from design to a deployed artifact, whose security rests on the integrity of each link.
tags: [security, supply-chain]
---

A **software supply chain** is the chain of activities, organizations, and components — source code, third-party dependencies, build tooling, registries, and update channels — that carries a program from design through development, build, distribution, acquisition, deployment, and maintenance to its end users, nested inside the broader information-and-communications-technology (ICT) supply chain. Each link is a point where code can be introduced or altered, so the chain's security rests on the integrity of every individual activity rather than any single one.
